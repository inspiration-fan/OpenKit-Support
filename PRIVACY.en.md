# OpenKit Privacy Policy

Updated October 16, 2026 · [简体中文](PRIVACY.zh-Hans.md)

OpenKit requires no account and includes no advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking SDKs. Contact: [yifannir@outlook.com](mailto:yifannir@outlook.com).

**On-device processing.** Imported files are stored as local copies. Viewing, extraction, editing, and text tools run on your device. OpenKit does not upload files, contents, archive passwords, or usage records to the developer. Your library, preferences, allowance, and purchase status stay locally; no developer server syncs them. Archive passwords are used for the current operation without a separate password vault.

**Sharing and backups.** Services receive files when you choose to import, export, or share. Cloud providers, system previews, device backups, and sharing destinations follow their own policies and your system settings.

**Apple purchases.** The App Store handles payment. OpenKit uses Apple-provided transactions locally to verify and restore Pro. We do not receive payment-card details or send transactions to a developer server. Manage subscriptions in your Apple Account. See [Apple’s privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

**Deletion and support.** Delete files in the app. Recently deleted retains them for 30 days, with an option to delete permanently sooner. Manage external copies and backups separately. If you contact support, we use the email, description, and attachments you provide to address your request, retaining them as needed. Email us to request deletion. GitHub issues are public; do not attach private files or passwords. Repository visits are subject to [GitHub’s privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

We update this policy and its date when our data practices change.
